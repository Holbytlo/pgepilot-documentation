- **Priorita 1 (1-2 tydny)**: instalace, nastaveni, sync migrace, forecast integrace.

- **Priorita 3**: hardening -- zabezpeceni, zalohy, monitoring.
- **Priorita 4**: budoucnost -- dalsi rozsireni a pokrocile funkce.
